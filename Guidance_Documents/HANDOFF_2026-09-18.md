## 1. Standing rules (unchanged, and the ones people trip on)

1. **Register before spend.** Every experiment gets an addendum in the prereg with a dollar
   ceiling, a stop rule, readouts with baselines in brackets, pre-declared readings for every
   branch, and guards, BEFORE any model call. Results are appended as `## X RESULTS` blocks.
   Nothing above an appended block is ever edited (cancellation markers on headings are the one
   allowed exception, Addendum 6 precedent).
2. **Read mechanically.** Readings are applied to the pre-declared branches verbatim. A branch
   that fails is reported as failing. Under-gated readouts (< 60 fired) are reported as
   under-gated, never tabulated.
3. **The guard is a literal string.** `GUARD FAILED` printed anywhere means the numbers are
   unread until the guard is fixed and re-registered (Addendum 6 retraction; 16.11b; 16.14).
4. **Caches are keyed by NAME**, not content: scaffold / role id / arm / item / sample index /
   token cap (`run_crowdgold_deliberation.call_cache_path`). Every new role set needs new role
   ids and a new moderator prefix (`install()` pattern in `run_crowdgold_unembodied.py`,
   `run_crowdgold_topology.py`, `run_crowdgold_modvendor.py`). The parent-sha check guards
   REPLAY, not WRITES: a changed prompt under an old name overwrites the registered record
   (this is how the `--moderator-model` flag would have destroyed 2,520 registered r3/r4 records
   in 16.17; the wrapper exists for that reason).
5. **The base runner rewrites `<tag>_rows.csv` BEFORE its guard verdict** (L3583 vs L3620).
   Back up the CSVs before any `--samples N` re-run (done for the three Tier 2 tags as
   `<tag>_k1_{rows,votes}.csv` and `<tag>_k1_summary.json`).
6. **Every citation is fetched at a primary page.** The ARR rejection was for fabricated
   references. The ledger is `papers/embodied_sensor/related_works_verified.json`; the bib is
   `papers/embodied_sensor/references.bib` (239 entries as of 2026-09-14).
7. **Prose style for the paper:** no em dashes, no colons or semicolons in running prose, no
   lists, plain words; every number carries a `%` source comment pointing at a prereg RESULTS
   line or an artefact key. Clean (comment-free) copies sit beside each file (`*_clean.tex`).
8. **n-player games are future work** (PI, 2026-09-12). Do not build on TMGBench beyond 2x2.
9. **PI instruction on claims (2026-09-14):** "don't argue a stronger case than what we can
   directly justify."

## 2. What the programme established (the paper's four contributions, at the record's scope)

The paper is `papers/embodied_sensor/` (ICLR 2027 template; ARR/ACL is the fallback venue).
The story, in the order the introduction tells it:

**Embodiment is a control.** A copy of a model told whom it speaks for takes that side on 99.7
percent (writer) and 97.4 percent (other party) of opening statements (role-lock 0.971 on
grok). Narration, the light dose, cuts validation of the asker by 22 to 41 points across four
models but on a provably false premise raises affirmation from 15.7 to 22.3 percent on a first
pass; a second narration pass takes most of it back (16.14b: DiD -0.073 [-0.107, -0.039]);
narration is not what creates the collective's structure (16.16). Removing the stake but
keeping direction leaves lock at 0.946 (16.8).

**The collective is a graph; where the roles lock it has one effective voter.** The majority
of three grok seats equals the neutral seat on 1,642 of 1,642 debates; the CV oracle over the
three opening verdicts is +0.000 [-0.004, +0.000] (16.15.4). The optimal log-odds weighted rule
is the neutral dictator on grok and nano (aggregation_rules.json, unregistered). On haiku
(lock 0.44) the writer's advocate is the best seat (+0.102 [+0.028, +0.182]).
Roles x edges (16.10): lock, localisation and G3 survive cutting every seat-to-seat read; the
fire rate drops 0.196 -> 0.129; identical readers produce no structure and are MORE accurate
(0.872 vs 0.842); no cell beats its best seat by vote; every collective is below grok alone
(0.911). Narration removal (16.16) leaves lock 0.974, localisation +0.339, G3 +0.644, fire
0.131. The moderator's model sets the verdict over byte-identical locked seats: sonnet +0.038
[+0.014, +0.064], haiku -0.115 [-0.169, -0.060] (16.17). A cross-vendor decider panel does not
beat its best member at the opening statement, does at r2 (+0.031 [+0.005, +0.057]), and sits
below grok alone (16.18). A like-biased second advocate copies its principal (phi +0.975) and
pulls the verdict toward its side, accuracy -0.074, P(at fault) -0.121 (16.19). A third party's
advocate breaks the one-dimensional stake (16.21).

**The flag is a second objector, and it lives in one-loser verdicts.** The registered sensor
is the stake-blind counter, two or more of the three seats objecting at the label round (it
counts the neutral seat; 44 of the 93 one-loser firings include it). Within one-loser verdicts
(YTA/NTA) flagged debates are wrong 0.387 vs 0.039, 9.83x, +0.348 [+0.201, +0.493]; within
both-party verdicts (ESH/NAH) the lift is -0.097 and the verdict type itself is the cheaper
signal (16.15.1). Routing on the counter to a judge beats the collective (+0.038 haiku judge,
+0.070 sonnet judge) and, on the registered counter, beats grok solo by +0.024 [+0.005, +0.045]
(16.22, R1 HOLDS), BUT the solo's own three-sample disagreement routes as well (+0.002 [-0.019,
+0.024] against the counter) at 3 calls instead of 17, so the collective's flag is dominated
on cost for a solo model's errors (16.22 R3, R5). Transfer of the flag to other generators:
nano yes (+0.216 [+0.064, +0.383]), haiku no (16.15.3).

**The extent.** Silence on Scruples Dilemmas (6 of 624 fired); flooding on ESH/NAH (0.603 vs
0.072 fire); modest movement without a counterparty (BrokenMath: "lowered false affirmation by
abstaining more, not by rejecting more", 16.15.6). Games (16.13, 16.13c, 16.13d): under a
binding plan the solo solves the value region alone and the advocates barely lock; without
enforcement every node recommends the equilibrium (a one-shot PD's correlated-equilibrium set
is its Nash set); the mediator adds nothing over a naive reader; the collective adds restraint
where no plan is unique (+0.151 [+0.043, +0.280], planner arm, unreplicated) and coordination
on two-equilibrium games (5 to 6 of 27 vs a solo that declines on 27 of 27).

Cross-model grip table today: grok (the headline); nano floods (fire 0.762, one-loser lift
+0.031 [-0.027, +0.088]); haiku_fixed 100 items under-gated (+0.089 [-0.048, +0.251], 25
fired); sonnet 16 items (silent); gpt-4o UNREAD (content filter, 16.11b).
