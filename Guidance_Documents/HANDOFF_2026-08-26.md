## 2. What is ROBUST and still stands (safe to cite without re-checking)

- **ELEPHANT-OEQ validation drop.** NoT reduces judge-scored validation of the asker's own
  account by **22–41 percentage points**, corrected for a real 4,000-character truncation
  bug in the judge pipeline, across `claude-haiku-4-5` (−26.7), `claude-sonnet-4-6` (−22.3),
  `gpt-5.4-nano` (−41.4), `grok-4-1-fast-reasoning` (−36.0). This is a real, replicated,
  robust social/register effect. Reproduce with
  `python3 -m scripts.analyze_length_matched_elephant --corrected`.
- **Section attribution is real and clean (structural content, not sycophancy outcome).**
  `divergence_study_outputs/subinstruction_attribution.csv`, `claude-sonnet-4-6`, n=90/cell.
  Dropping Stakeholders kills stakeholder count (δ=−0.41) and nothing else. Dropping
  Uncertainty kills hedging (δ=−0.93) and nothing else. Dropping Consequences kills causal-hop
  depth (δ=−0.22). Off-diagonal effects never exceed |δ|=0.17. Protagonist and Commitment
  knockouts move nothing on any of these three metrics — no claim is licensed about those two
  sections. This is Figure 2 of the manuscript draft and it is fine as-is.
- **Aggregation is a variance operator and cannot correct a shared bias — derived AND
  measured.** Cross-vendor panels gain ~5x more accuracy from majority voting than
  same-model panels (diversity buys accuracy), while the sycophantic criterion shift passes
  through both essentially untouched (a bias is not variance, so voting doesn't cancel it).
  `scripts/analyze_aggregation_passthrough.py`. One flagged caveat (never resolved, see §6):
  this was computed on data that may share the ESH/shared-blame contamination described in
  §3 and should be rechecked on the binary instrument.
- **Deliberation converges by conformity, not synthesis.** Across 100 cached debates:
  unanimity 46%→63% across rounds, position changes land on an already-held position ~13
  points above the best defensible chance baseline (p=0.016), only ~30% of moves are
  genuinely novel, and stated confidence never anticipates an impending change of mind
  (movers 0.948 vs stayers 0.969 at round 0). `scripts/analyze_deliberation_dynamics.py`.
- **The published 5-round consensus protocol's terminal vote carries no signal.** Across 82
  debates, the joint vote distribution (unanimous / 1-reject / 2-reject / 3-reject) is
  statistically indistinguishable from three *independent* coin flips at the observed
  marginal rate. The "95% consensus" headline is real but uninformative about whether
  deliberation did anything.

## 3. What was RETRACTED this session (do not cite without the correction attached)

- **R1 (compliance-gated mechanism) — retracted.** The original claim that models which
  visibly *comply* with the scaffold's format show a larger effect than non-compliant ones
  turned out to be a complete-case artefact: non-compliant responses were disproportionately
  ones the judge failed to score at all, and once that's handled with a Manski bound the
  effect is not distinguishable from zero, and its sign is heterogeneous across generators.
  Full writeup and the paper edits are already committed (`cae4968`, `78be8a0`, `2ed5a67`).
- **The core Crowd-Gold sycophancy instrument (5-way ESH verdict) was measuring an
  artefact, not blame-shifting.** This is the session's single biggest methodological
  finding. Offered a 5-way verdict set (YTA/NTA/ESH/NAH/UNRESOLVED), models were shown to be
  using the "ESH" (everyone's somewhat at fault) category as a hedge, and swapping between
  ESH and NTA under stance pressure — *not* changing who they actually blamed. When the
  instrument was rebuilt to force a genuinely binary, comparative verdict (see `4d7364e`,
  "Make the binary choice coherent, not merely forced — ESH attempts 24 → 0"), the previously
  significant sycophancy effect on that corpus **evaporated** (`3507932`, "E1: a clean null"),
  and so did the scaffold's apparent *collateral* damage on neutral items (`a7579d5`, "E3:
  null, and it takes the collateral finding with it"). **Any Crowd-Gold-based sycophancy or
  collateral-damage claim from before commit `d9527ea` is void unless re-verified on the
  binary instrument.**
- **The E2/E3 section-ablation-on-sycophancy-outcome result is void, not just weak.** A
  powered (k=3) rerun of the section ablation, but scored against the *sycophancy* DV
  (criterion shift) rather than structural content, found no section knockout individually
  restores sycophancy (4 of 5 knockouts null), and the two arms that did show something
  (dropping Commitment; the one-line baseline) both point toward *more* resistance than the
  full scaffold — the opposite of what an "elaborate structure matters" story predicts. This
  result is now further undermined by the point above: the underlying Crowd-Gold sycophancy
  signal it was decomposing has since been shown to be substantially an ESH artefact.

## 6. Process notes / house rules that were established or re-confirmed this session

- Every quantitative claim that matters gets an item-clustered bootstrap CI, not a raw
  point estimate. Several retractions this session happened specifically because an earlier
  result had never been given one.
- When an instrument offers a hedge/shared-fault category, check whether that category is
  absorbing the effect before trusting any result from it (this is *the* lesson of §3).
- Missingness (noverdict/noncommittal responses) gets a Manski bound, not silent exclusion,
  whenever it's plausible the missingness correlates with the arm.
- Style constraint the PI gave for manuscript prose: **no em dashes, no colons or semicolons
  in running prose, vary sentence length.** Applies to `papers/scaffold_results/` content.
- Commit messages in this repo are long and explicit about what changed and why (see `git
  log` above for the house style) — keep doing that, it's clearly been load-bearing for
  continuity across sessions.
- Never push to any remote without being asked. Never commit to `main`.
