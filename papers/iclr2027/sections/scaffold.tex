\label{sec-scaffold}

protagonist, stakeholders, consequences, uncertainty and decision, before
answering, and its control is the one-line CoT prompt, both verbatim in

Following the social account of objectivity
\citep{longino1990science,popper2011open}, the scaffold has the
model write out the perspectives missing from one person's account of a
conflict, and the collective of Section~\ref{sec-collective} places them in
separate agents with opposed interests.
% "the perspectives missing from one person's account" restates the introduction's "a model answering
% one person's account holds only that person's perspective" (sections/introduction.tex); the earlier
% "into the trace before the answer" is carried by "before answering" in the first sentence
% Citation matches the introduction's for the same account (longino1990science,popper2011open);
% mercier2011argumentative stays cited in Related Work for the argumentative theory
Among related prompts, SimToM \citep{wilf2024simtom} first restates what
one character knows and then answers from it, the chain-of-thought of
\citet{jin22_moralcot} is built from a theory of moral reasoning, and
multi-persona self-collaboration \citep{wang2024spp} has one model play
several personas. NoT instead fixes five content sections and a closing
commitment to a decision in one system prompt, applied once to the unchanged
query without worked examples.

\fbox{\begin{minipage}{0.94\columnwidth}\scriptsize
\textbf{Narration-of-Thought (system prompt).}
You are a thoughtful advisor. When given an ethical dilemma, reason through it
as a five-part first-person narrative before giving your answer.\\[2pt]
Section 1 -- Protagonist: Name and briefly characterise the decision-maker (who
they are, their role, what they know).\\
Section 2 -- Stakeholders: List every person whose life intersects this
decision and state what is at stake for each.\\
Section 3 -- Consequences: For each available action, narrate its consequences
at least two steps into the future for each stakeholder.\\
Section 4 -- Uncertainty: State what remains genuinely uncertain about each
projected future.\\
Section 5 -- Decision: Commit to a specific decision and explain, within the
narrative frame, why that trajectory is preferable to the alternatives.\\[2pt]
Work through all five sections before giving your final answer.\\[4pt]
\textbf{Standard chain-of-thought (control).} You are a helpful assistant. Think
step by step, then give your answer.
\end{minipage}}
