\paragraph{Sycophancy is a trained bias, and it is steerable.}
Preference-tuned models grow more sycophantic with scale and user confidence
\citep{perez2023discovering,sharma23}, validate the asker's own account of a
conflict \citep{cheng25elephant} and revise correct answers under pushback
\citep{fanous2025syceval,malmqvist24}. Synthetic fine-tuning data reduce
sycophancy \citep{wei2023synthetic}. The closest prior idea to NoT is
persona vectors \citep{chen2025persona}, directions in activation space that
track traits such as sycophancy and can be read to monitor, or
moved to steer, the persona a model adopts. An off-the-shelf sceptical
persona vector recovers most of what steering along a sycophancy direction
\citep{rimsky2024caa} achieves \citep{kelkar2026devil}. NoT is their
prompt-level counterpart, assigning the model a stance toward the asker and
the other parties without editing activations, and a sceptical-persona
prompt, the one-line version of that move, lowers validation more than NoT
under four of five judges (Section~\ref{sec-single}).

\citep{shanahan2023roleplay,li2023camel}, although persona prompts amplify sycophancy when
agreeable \citep{shah2026toonice} and carry other costs (Appendix~\ref{app-related}),

\paragraph{Debate places truth at the judge, not in the debaters.}
Scalable oversight puts opposed agents before a judge \citep{irving18}, who gains more from a debate than from one advocate
\citep{michael2023debate,khan2024debating}, and gains over answering alone only
when it lacks the debaters' information \citep{kenton2024scalable}.
% clarity loop 3 (Related Work reviewer, 2026-09-24): "gains" restores the verb of the Kenton clause, same claim
Debate among copies of one model, as in our collective, helps some
benchmarks \citep{du23,liang2024divergent,chan2023chateval} and on others
\citep{smit2024mad,wang2024rethinking} fails to beat self-consistency
\citep{wang2023selfconsistency}, a majority vote over one model's samples like
our solo, as debate converges to the existing
majority \citep{estornell2024multillm}, homogeneous
agents with uniform updates cannot reliably improve expected correctness
\citep{zhu2026demystifying}, and debate alone leaves it unchanged where voting
raises it \citep{choi2025debateorvote}.

\paragraph{Argument and social objectivity motivate the design.}
% clarity loop 3 (Related Work reviewer, 2026-09-24, related-07): the heading names the two accounts, the
% argumentative theory (mercier2011argumentative) and the social account of objectivity (the Introduction's
% term, longino1990science, popper2011open), in place of "Two accounts of reasoning as social"; shorter, same
% scope. Its "Beyond both accounts" edit lapsed with the Aumann sentence (whole-14), and the three payments it

Human reasoning may have evolved for argument, with biased advocacy from
individuals and truth emerging when others evaluate it
\citep{mercier2011argumentative,mercier2017enigma}, since arguments rather
than confidence move groups \citep{trouche2014arguments} while individuals are
lazy about their own arguments \citep{trouche2016selective}, though the group
claim has the weaker evidence \citep{mercier2016predictions}.
Assigned devil's advocacy helps less than authentic dissent
\citep{nemeth2001devil}, though structured conflict beats consensus
\citep{schweiger1986group,schwenk1990effects}. Objectivity is a
property of a community's structure of criticism rather than of any member
\citep{longino1990science,longino2002fate,popper2011open}, a structure the
scaffold places inside one model's written reasoning and the collective across agents.
