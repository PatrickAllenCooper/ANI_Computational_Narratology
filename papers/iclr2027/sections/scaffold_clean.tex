\section{The Scaffold}
\label{sec-scaffold}

Figure~\ref{fig:prompt} gives NoT and its control, the one-line CoT prompt,
verbatim.
Under the scaffold's principle, organised criticism across perspectives, NoT
has the model write out the perspectives missing from one person's account
of a conflict, and the collective of Section~\ref{sec-collective} gives them
to separate, opposed agents.
Sections~\ref{sec-single} and~\ref{sec-collective} use the NoT text
unchanged on every model and task, with what a task adds, such as labels for
who is at fault or a seat's role, after it in the system prompt or user turn
(Appendices~\ref{app-pushback}, \ref{app-seat} and~\ref{app-prompts}).
The solo and the stronger judge, sonnet, answer instead under a brief
unscaffolded prompt, which gives those labels and asks for at
most four sentences of reasoning (Appendix~\ref{app-prompts}).
Among related prompts, SimToM \citep{wilf2024simtom} first restates what
one character knows, then answers from it, MoralCoT \citep{jin22_moralcot}
builds chain-of-thought on a theory of moral reasoning, and Solo
Performance Prompting \citep{wang2024spp} has one model play several personas.
NoT instead fixes its five sections in one system prompt used in one call per
answer, with no restating pass or worked examples.
NoT also abbreviates Narrative-of-Thought
\citep{zhang2024narrativeofthought}, a temporal-reasoning prompt sharing
neither task nor content with ours.

\textbf{Narration-of-Thought (system prompt).}
You are a thoughtful advisor. When given an ethical dilemma, reason through it
as a five-part first-person narrative before giving your answer.\\[2pt]
Section 1 -- Protagonist: Name and briefly characterise the decision-maker (who
they are, their role, what they know).\\
Section 2 -- Stakeholders: List every person whose life intersects this
decision and state what is at stake for each.\\
Section 3 -- Consequences: For each available action, narrate its consequences
at least two steps into the future for each stakeholder.\\
Section 4 -- Uncertainty: State what remains genuinely uncertain about each
projected future.\\
Section 5 -- Decision: Commit to a specific decision and explain, within the
narrative frame, why that trajectory is preferable to the alternatives.\\[2pt]
Work through all five sections before giving your final answer.\\[4pt]
\textbf{Standard chain-of-thought (system prompt, control).} You are a helpful assistant. Think
step by step, then give your answer.
