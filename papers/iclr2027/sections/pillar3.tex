\label{sec-collective}

\paragraph{Instrument and protocol.}
Disputes are Scruples Anecdotes \citep{lourie2021scruples}, first-person
conflict accounts whose crowd verdict, a community judgement of blame rather
than a ground truth, is gold when at least 90 percent of at least 50 votes
agree.
% Scruples citation added 2026-09-23 from https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/17589
% and https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.09094, both fetched 2026-09-23
% scripts/run_crowdgold_aita.py (--min-votes 50, --min-consensus 0.90), prereg E4 ~l.294-312
ELEPHANT's own split of such posts has no vote counts, so it admits no
consensus rule, and our earlier validation claim on it was withdrawn
(Appendix~\ref{app-prereg}).

\paragraph{Dissent flags errors.}
A verdict that blames one party gives that party's advocate a reason to
object, so the informative objection is a second one, which no assigned
side explains \citep{milgrom1986relying,lipman1995robust}, and the counter
is a fixed threshold, not a learned rule \citep{he2026minoritysentinel}.
% W40 (2026-09-23): He et al. 2026 (arXiv 2606.29270, https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.29270, abstract fetched
% 2026-09-23) train a LightGBM classifier on a debate fingerprint from debate logs of three heterogeneous
% agents with no assigned side to decide when to flip a majority vote to the minority, and find an
% LLM-as-judge baseline gives negative net gain; the counter is a fixed threshold (n_objectors >= 2,
% prereg 16.1 ~l.3042-3084) on objections to the synthesis and routes to a judge. "The roles fix" the
% split on grok: majority of opening verdicts equals the neutral seat's on 1,642 of 1,642 debates
% (effective_voter.json, prereg 16.15.4 RESULTS ~l.4496). Existing bib key, no new citation.

the debates where a second seat objects against 0.039 where at most one
does, 9.8 times as often, a lift of $+34.8$ points $[+20.1, +49.3]$,
against 4.4 times pooled over all its verdicts.
