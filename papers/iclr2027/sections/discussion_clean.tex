\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec-discussion}

\paragraph{What the evidence supports.}
We set out to test whether a first-person narrative of the missing
perspectives lowers sycophancy.
On judged validation, where we separated the scaffold's content from that
form, and in the collective, where we removed the scaffold from the seats,
the evidence supports only its principle, organised criticism across
perspectives \citep{longino1990science}, in a weaker, judge-dependent form
than we set out to test, so the name Narration-of-Thought records that
hypothesis, not the mechanism we found.
Pooled over four generators, a third-person checklist of NoT's five
sections lowers judged validation below CoT more than NoT under every
judge, and the narrative instruction without the sections raises it above
CoT (Table~\ref{tab-form-main}), though part of the checklist's effect may be
a cooler register, which this design cannot separate from less
endorsement.
In the collective a second objection flags errors on four of five models,
but objections come from assigned opposition, which costs accuracy on grok
in a contrast not declared in advance (Section~\ref{sec-collective}), and
plain CoT seats beat narrated ones on grok and Llama. The collective is
less accurate than the same model alone on four of the five models of
Table~\ref{tab-routing}, indistinguishable from it on haiku, and even with
its flagged debates sent to sonnet beats it only on Mistral.

\paragraph{Judges and what they measure.}
The single-agent reduction holds on all seven generators only under the
production judge, haiku, a generator sharing a developer with sonnet,
whose reduction no other judge confirms, and under all five judges only on
Llama (Table~\ref{tab-claims}, Section~\ref{sec-single}).
No judge panel can separate register from endorsement, but the judge-free
pushback test \citep{fanous2025syceval} separates it from commitment.
There NoT gives up its verdict more often than CoT whether the user
disputes it or just asks again, carried almost entirely by Llama, and the
registered net contrast is null because the two rises are alike, not
because either is absent.
On Llama and Mistral the verdict's shift toward excusing the user as writer
also survives NoT, and DeepSeek shows none either way.

\paragraph{Other limitations.}
Each generator answered each item once per prompt in each run, so only its
two to four runs, compared after the fact (Appendix~\ref{app-rep4k}), show
how far the reduction moves between generations. They keep it under the
production judge, with an interval excluding zero, in every run but
sonnet's second at 4,096 tokens, and shrink it on three generator-judge
pairs.
Content filters on most platforms refuse some disputes, so the collective
and pushback item sets differ by model.

\paragraph{Future work.}
The decisive test crosses first- and third-person voice with narrative and
checklist form and has blind human raters score endorsement apart from
register. A second is whether any form of the content resists pushback
over several turns \citep{hong2025sycophancy}. A third is whether dissent
flags errors where seats lock weakly to their sides and routes them better
than disagreement among the solo model's three answers
\citep{jitkrittum2023deferral,manakul2023selfcheck}, which routed errors at
least as well everywhere but Mistral, in comparisons registered only on
grok (Appendix~\ref{app-collective}).
