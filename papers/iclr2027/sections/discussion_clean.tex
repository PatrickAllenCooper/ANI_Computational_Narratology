\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec-discussion}

\paragraph{What the evidence supports.}
On judged validation and in the collective, the two settings in which we
separated the scaffold's content from its form, the evidence supports its
principle, organised criticism across perspectives, only in a weaker form
than we set out to test, and not its narrative packaging.
Pooled over four generators, a third-person checklist of the same five
elements lowers validation more than NoT under each of the five judges and
the narrative instruction alone raises it (Table~\ref{tab-form-main}),
though the checklist may score lower partly for a cooler register, which
this design cannot separate from less endorsement.
In the collective the objections routing uses come from assigned
opposition, which costs accuracy on grok (Section~\ref{sec-collective}),
plain chain-of-thought seats beat narrated ones on both models tested, the
collective is below the same model answering alone on four of the five
models of Table~\ref{tab-routing} and indistinguishable from it on haiku,
and the routed collective beats the solo only on Mistral.

\paragraph{Judges and the construct.}
The single-agent reduction depends on the judge, holding on all seven
generators only under the production judge and under all five judges only
on Llama (Table~\ref{tab-claims}, with the Holm-corrected counts in
Section~\ref{sec-single}).
The production judge is also a generator and shares its developer with
sonnet, whose reduction no other judge confirms (Section~\ref{sec-single}).
No judge panel can separate register from endorsement, but the judge-free
pushback test of Section~\ref{sec-single} \citep{fanous2025syceval}
separates register from commitment, and there the scaffold is worse on both
raw measures, carried almost entirely by Llama, and its registered net
contrast, $+5.2$ points $[-1.7, +12.2]$, is null because the rises are
alike, not because either is absent (Section~\ref{sec-single}).
On Llama and Mistral NoT also keeps the verdict's shift toward the user as
writer, and DeepSeek shows none (Section~\ref{sec-single}).

\paragraph{Other limitations.}
Each generator answered each item once per prompt in each run, and the two
to four runs per generator, compared after the fact
(Appendix~\ref{app-rep4k}), keep the production judge's reduction with an
interval excluding zero on every generator in every run except sonnet's
second run at 4,096 tokens ($-4.7$ $[-13.3, +3.3]$) and shrink it on three
generator and judge pairs, while content filters removed disputes each
platform refuses, so the collective and pushback panels differ across
models.

\paragraph{Future work.}
The decisive test crosses first- and third-person voice with narrative and
checklist form and has blind human raters score endorsement apart from
warmth. Another is whether any form of the content resists pushback over
several turns \citep{hong2025sycophancy}. A third is whether dissent flags errors where seats lock weakly to their sides
and routes them better than a model's own sample disagreement, the cascade
baseline
\citep{jitkrittum2023deferral,manakul2023selfcheck}, which matched or beat
it everywhere but Mistral in mostly exploratory readouts
(Appendix~\ref{app-collective}).
On judged validation and in the collective the evidence supports the
principle of organised criticism across perspectives
\citep{longino1990science} only in this weaker, judge-dependent form, so the
name Narration-of-Thought records the hypothesis we tested, that a
first-person narrative carries the reduction, and not the mechanism we found.
