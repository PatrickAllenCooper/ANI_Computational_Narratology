\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec-discussion}

\paragraph{What the evidence supports.}
We set out to test whether a first-person narrative of the missing
perspectives lowers sycophancy.
On judged validation and in the collective the evidence supports only the
scaffold's principle, organised criticism across perspectives
\citep{longino1990science}, in a weaker, judge-dependent form, so the name
Narration-of-Thought records that hypothesis, not what we found.
Pooled over four generators and under every judge, a third-person
checklist of NoT's five sections lowers judged validation further below CoT
than NoT does, and the narrative instruction without the sections raises it
above CoT (Table~\ref{tab-form-main}), though part of the checklist's effect
may be a cooler register, which this design cannot separate from less
endorsement.
In the collective, among verdicts that blame one party, a second objection
flags errors on four of five models, a reading proposed after grok's debates
were collected. Those objections, however, come from assigned opposition,
which on grok costs accuracy compared with three neutral seats in a contrast
not pre-registered (Section~\ref{sec-collective}).
CoT seats make the collective more accurate than NoT seats on grok and Llama.
The collective is less accurate than the same model answering alone, the
solo, on four of the five models of Table~\ref{tab-routing},
indistinguishable from it on haiku, and even with its flagged debates sent
to sonnet beats it only on Mistral.

\paragraph{Judges and judge-free tests.}
The single-agent reduction holds on all seven generators only under the
production judge, haiku, and under all five judges only on Llama, while no
other judge confirms it on sonnet, which shares haiku's developer
(Table~\ref{tab-claims}, Section~\ref{sec-single}).
No judge panel can separate register from endorsement, but the judge-free
pushback test \citep{fanous2025syceval} measures commitment, keeping a
verdict when asked to reconsider, without scoring tone.
There NoT gives up its verdict more often than CoT whether the user
disputes it or just asks again, carried almost entirely by Llama, and the
registered primary, the first rise net of the second, is null because the
two rises are alike, not because either is absent.
In a second judge-free test, against a brief unscaffolded prompt rather
than CoT, NoT does not remove the verdict's shift toward excusing the user
as writer on Llama and Mistral, and DeepSeek shows none under either prompt.

\paragraph{Other limitations.}
Each generator answered each item once per prompt per run, and our
intervals resample items, not runs, so only its two to four runs, compared
post hoc (Table~\ref{tab-app-runs}), show how far the reduction moves
between generations.
They keep it under the production judge, with an
interval excluding zero, in every run but sonnet's second at 4,096 tokens,
and the regeneration shrinks it on sonnet under the production judge and on
Mistral and DeepSeek under gpt-4o.
Content filters on most platforms refuse some disputes, so the collective
and pushback item sets differ by model.

\paragraph{Future work.}
The decisive test crosses first- and third-person voice with narrative and
checklist form and has blind human raters score endorsement apart from
register.
A second is whether any form of the content resists pushback
over several turns \citep{hong2025sycophancy}. A third is whether dissent
flags errors where seats lock weakly to their sides and
routes them better than disagreement among the solo's three answers
\citep{jitkrittum2023deferral,manakul2023selfcheck}, which routed errors at
least as well everywhere but Mistral, in comparisons registered only on
grok (Appendix~\ref{app-collective}).
