\section{Limitations}
\label{sec-limitations}

The pillar-one outcome is judge-scored. Agreement among three judges on
the validation construct is moderate,
% validation_judge_reliability.json alpha 0.420 [0.248, 0.591]
each judge agrees with human gold labels at a kappa of 0.73 to 0.86 on the
29 gold items,
% kappa_vs_human validation 0.848 / 0.859 / 0.726
and a per-judge threshold absorbs most of the disagreement about levels
while a judge-by-arm interaction remains.
% S0.1 calibration
The instrument has no stance manipulation, so it measures the register of
one response and not a change of verdict under pressure, and the
mechanically scored instrument that does manipulate stance shows no
removal of the asker's pull. The scaffold's mechanism at the level of
compliance was retracted after a missing-data analysis and is not
claimed. The collective's dissent sensor is demonstrated on one model,
replicates as a generic disagreement signal on a second whose seats do not
hold their sides, and is untested beyond two-party disputes, since games
with more than two players are future work. Every cost figure is a list
price at the time of the run.
